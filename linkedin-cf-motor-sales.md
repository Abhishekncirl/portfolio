## 2. LinkedIn post (story)

**Post text (paste as-is, add 1–2 screenshots or the live-site link preview)**

🚗 Just shipped: a full-stack website and admin platform for an independent Irish car dealership, CF Motor Sales.

The brief was simple on paper. The dealership imports UK and Japanese vehicles, sells car parts, and offers finance. They needed a modern website for buyers, and an admin system the owner could run themselves without touching code. Tight hosting budget. Full self-management. Mobile-first.

Over three months (August to October 2026), I delivered the project end to end:

→ Discovery and requirements with the client
→ Brand-led UI and a Tailwind design system
→ React and TypeScript front-end with routing, code-splitting, and image optimisation
→ Firebase backend (Firestore, Auth, Storage) with security rules
→ A full admin CMS to manage vehicles, parts, photos, finance figures, and reviews
→ A multi-step finance application with a live Hire Purchase and APR calculator
→ WhatsApp deep-link enquiries that pre-fill the vehicle and price
→ CI/CD to GitHub Pages, custom domain, and HTTPS

The platform is now live at www.cfmotorsales.com. The owner adds a new car in under a minute, moderates reviews from their phone, and updates finance rates without ever opening a line of code. Running costs stay minimal because the whole thing runs on a static CDN with a serverless Firebase backend.

Biggest takeaway? Writing clear requirements up front saved weeks later. Every content type, every admin action, every finance rule was agreed before a single component was built. That is where the Business Analyst mindset earns its keep, even on a build project.

If you are a small business owner wondering whether you need a developer every time you want to change your website: you do not. A proper CMS, built around how you actually work, pays for itself in week one.

Big thanks to the CF Motor Sales team for the trust and the clear brief.

## Tips when posting

- Lead with the hero banner screenshot from your portfolio hero, or a clean shot of the live homepage.
- A **native carousel** (3–5 images) outperforms a single screenshot: homepage → showroom → admin CMS → finance calculator → outcome summary.
- Post on **Tuesday or Wednesday morning (Dublin time)** for the best reach.
- Pin the post to your profile for a week after posting.
- Add the project to the **Projects** section of your profile the same day, so visitors from the post land on structured proof.
